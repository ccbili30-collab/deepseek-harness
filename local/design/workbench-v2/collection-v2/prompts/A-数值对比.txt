SCREEN A — "where the money came from" proportional composition. Top of canvas: page title "政治经济动态表（1127–1141）" and a small segmented control "版式：体量 | 图鉴 | 长卷" with 体量 selected. Hero row: two big comparison blocks side by side — a small block labeled "南宋渡江初 岁入" with a huge number "1000万缗" and a much larger pale block labeled "北宋熙丰 岁入" with "6000万缗", visually showing the 1:6 ratio as area. Below: a treemap-like mosaic of rounded tiles whose AREA is proportional to revenue, each tile filled with a painted illustration and a big number overlaid in white: largest "盐利 1920万" (salt pans by the sea), "折帛钱 1700万" (bolts of silk), "酒课 1400万" (a wine workshop with jars), "总制钱 780万", "经制钱 660万", "月桩钱 400万", "茶引 270万" (tea terraces), smallest "上供正赋 200万". A thin caption line under the mosaic: "正赋仅占小头，大头全靠军兴新税与专卖". Right edge of canvas: a slim vertical strip of three small text cards for non-numeric entries: "宰相走马灯", "五护军", "绍兴和议".
